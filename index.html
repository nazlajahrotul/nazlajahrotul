Cosmic Gallery

Celestial objects and fascinating facts from the universe

Earth

Earth appears blue from space not only because of water, but also because its atmosphere scatters blue light efficiently.

Mars

Mars is known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface, hinting at a past that may have once supported water.

Jupiter

Jupiter is so massive that it protects inner planets by pulling asteroids away with its strong gravity.

Nebula

Nebulae are stellar nurseries where new stars are born from clouds of gas and cosmic dust.
