ABOUT THIS SPACE

Aitorunoct is a personal cosmic art project. It is not meant to explain the universe, but to feel it — slowly, quietly, in shades of deep blue and distant light.

FACT I — WE ARE STARDUST

Almost every element in the human body was forged inside stars billions of years ago. When massive stars died, they scattered the building blocks of life across the universe.

FACT II — THE SOUND OF SPACE

Space itself is silent, but galaxies and black holes emit vibrations that can be translated into sound. The universe has a rhythm — we just need the right instruments to hear it.

FACT III — LOOKING BACK IN TIME

When we observe distant galaxies, we are seeing them as they were millions or even billions of years ago. Every telescope is a time machine, pointing into the past.

FACT IV — THE BLUE PLANET

Earth appears blue from space not only because of water, but because our atmosphere scatters blue light more efficiently. A fragile color, protecting life.
